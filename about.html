Our story

Ghar Ki Handi is built around a familiar feeling: coming back after a long day and wishing dinner tasted a bit like home. So we put together comforting handi, rice and meetha for students who want a proper, freshly made meal without leaving campus.

Every order is prepared with care and delivered to your hostel. Choose what sounds good, and we’ll take it from there. Shukriya for making room at your table for us.

Good to know

A few FAQs

Which hostels do you deliver to?

We deliver to all LUMS hostels. Add your hostel and room number when placing your order.

When are you open?

We’re open every day from 7:00 pm to 1:00 am.

How much is delivery?

Delivery is Rs 100, and it’s free when your food subtotal is Rs 1,500 or more.

Can I adjust the spice?

Check the 🌶️ spice level shown with each dish to find the heat that feels right for you.

How do I place an order?

Choose dishes on the menu, then fill in the order form with your hostel details and 11-digit phone number starting with 03.
